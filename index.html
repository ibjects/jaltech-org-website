Questions, answered.

What does JALTech do?

JALTech is a software house specializing in fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG). We build custom internal software, Android sales apps connected to inventory, distributor portals and consumer campaign platforms. Scan-and-win and management-in-training (MIT) apps are examples of what we build. We also deliver mall stalls and interactive brand activations.

Can you build internal Android apps connected to inventory?

Yes. We have built custom Android apps for internal sales teams connected to inventory. We can tailor the app to your team’s sales workflows and integrate it with the systems your business uses.

Can you manage both the software and a mall activation?

Yes. We can bring together campaign planning, the physical brand experience and its digital tools, including registration, QR journeys, games and reporting. The scope is agreed around your campaign and venue.

Do you only work with FMCG companies?

FMCG is our specialty. We also provide mobile and web development, MVP development, DevOps and cloud solutions, game development, and UI/UX design for businesses in other industries.

Can you work with our existing software?

Yes. We review your current systems and available APIs, then plan the integrations, data flows and access controls needed for the project.

How much will our project cost, and how long will it take?

Cost and timing depend on the scope, integrations and launch requirements. Share your goal and target date so we can propose a delivery plan and estimate.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance, cloud support and product improvements. Support scope and response expectations are agreed as part of your engagement.

What can we build for your business?

Tell us what you want to achieve, what is holding you back and when you need to launch. We’ll help define the next step.
